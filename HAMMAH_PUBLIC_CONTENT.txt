Brand: SL by Hammah

==================================================
GLOBAL CONTENT PRINCIPLES
==================================================

SL by Hammah should sound like a real fashion brand run by real people.

The voice should be:
- direct
- calm
- specific
- confident without sounding self-important
- premium without sounding distant
- contemporary without sounding trendy for the sake of it

Avoid generic fashion phrases such as:
- elevate your wardrobe
- timeless elegance
- where tradition meets modernity
- redefining luxury
- crafted for the modern individual
- style without limits
- experience sophistication
- discover unparalleled craftsmanship

Do not invent:
- final prices
- exact fabric compositions
- sourcing claims
- sustainability claims
- production methods
- founder history
- release dates
- delivery times
- return conditions
- cultural meanings behind prints
- guarantees

Approved positioning line:
“Considered essentials, cut for people who don’t dress for anyone else.”

Product universe:
- African Print Trousers — available at MVP launch
- Kaftans — future category
- African-made Footwear — future category

Registered members are called:
Hamatees

Member environment:
The Hammah Legacy

FINAL AI-AGENT CONTENT RULES
==================================================

When implementing this content:

1. Do not replace approved copy with generic marketing language.
2. Do not invent facts to “make the page feel complete”.
3. Keep placeholders where operational or legal information is not yet approved.
4. Do not turn every short statement into a paragraph.
5. Keep line breaks intentional in large editorial headlines.
6. Preserve the distinction between:
   - current orderable trousers
   - future kaftans
   - future African-made footwear
7. Never create fake products for future categories.
8. Never create fake final prices.
9. Never make unapproved claims about fabric, sourcing, sustainability, craftsmanship or manufacturing.
10. The copy should feel human, restrained and modern.
11. The public website should feel like a fashion house, not a software product.
12. The Hammah Legacy should feel like belonging, not a generic account dashboard.
13. WhatsApp is a continuation channel, not the place where the order begins.
14. Order tracking language should be simple and operational.
15. Legal pages remain structured working drafts until formally reviewed.
